# Unit 5 — Leadership Finding

## Understanding Rider No-Shows and Drivers With No Trips

**Finding**

In Assignment 4, we identified Drivers 2 and 3 as having higher rider no-show rates than the platform average of 6%. Driver 2 had 3 no-shows out of 16 trips (18.75%), while Driver 3 had 2 out of 13 trips (15.38%).

By joining the tables in Assignment 5, we learned more about these drivers. Driver 2 earned the Safe Driving badge, while Driver 3 has a high rating of 4.91 but is currently inactive. These details provide more context, but they do not explain why riders missed their trips. The number of no-shows is also small, so we should avoid making assumptions about driver performance.

Our outer joins also identified Drivers 14 and 15, who have no recorded trips. These drivers were missing from the INNER JOIN results, showing how reports can overlook people with no activity.

**Recommendation**

I recommend reviewing the missed rides associated with Drivers 2 and 3 to see whether pickup locations, scheduling, or communication issues might explain the higher no-show rates. We should also investigate why Driver 3 is inactive, without assuming that this is related to rider no-shows.

For Drivers 14 and 15, leadership should check their onboarding and availability status to understand why they have no recorded trips and whether they need support.

These findings show two different situations: drivers associated with higher rider no-show rates and drivers with no recorded activity. Investigating each group separately would help leadership understand platform performance and make better decisions.
